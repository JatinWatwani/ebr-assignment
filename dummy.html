Setup Guides

  1. Download the Lumora mobile application.
  2. Connect the device to a standard power outlet.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to pair the device with your local Wi-Fi network.

Our smart devices are designed for intuitive installation. For detailed configuration options and advanced integrations, please refer to the comprehensive user manual provided in the packaging.
